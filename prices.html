Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking.

Our packages are split into three tiers: Bronze, Silver, and Gold across three service categories; Photography, Videography and Photography & Videography combined. Each tier increases in coverage time, deliverables, and rounds of revisions. We also offer a Mini Shoot for quick, lightweight sessions. All prices are fixed per session and exclude transport costs, any travel required will be quoted separately before you confirm.

Yes, all packages can be adjusted to suit your specific needs. If you need more edited photos, extra reel revisions, drone coverage, or a different coverage duration, just let us know when you enquire and we'll put together a custom quote. Our Add-On options (listed under each category) are a good starting point, but we're always open to building something from scratch if none of the standard tiers quite fit.

We accept EFT (electronic funds transfer), direct bank deposit, and in-person card payments via Yoco including Apple Pay tap-to-pay. A 50% non-refundable deposit is required to secure your booking, with the remaining balance due before the shoot date.

Delivery timelines are agreed before your shoot so you always know what to expect. If you need your content turned around faster than the standard timeline, a rush delivery option is available for an additional 20% fee. Raw, unedited files are not included in any package unless specifically agreed to upfront.

Cancellations more than 10 days before the shoot date incur no additional charge beyond the non-refundable deposit. Cancellations within 10 days attract an additional fee of 20% of the outstanding balance. Each booking allows one free reschedule, your payment transfers to the new date, subject to availability. All cancellations must be submitted in writing.

FOM Studios retains the right to use content for portfolio and marketing purposes, including our website and social media, unless a confidentiality agreement is arranged in writing before the shoot. If you'd prefer your content stays private, just let us know during the booking process and we'll accommodate that.

Yes, all packages exclude transport costs, which are quoted separately based on the shoot location. Get in touch with your shoot details and we'll include a transport quote along with your package confirmation.
